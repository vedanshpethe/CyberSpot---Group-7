THINK BEFORE YOU TRUST

Real vs Fake Messages

Compare examples and learn what makes a message suspicious.

Learn to pause and inspect before you respond.

SUSPICIOUS MESSAGE
"Your account will be blocked today! Click this unknown link and share your OTP to continue."
Warning signs
  • Creates urgency
  • Requests a one-time code
  • Uses an unfamiliar link
MORE TRUSTWORTHY APPROACH
"There is an update available. Open the official app or type the service address yourself to review it."
Safer signs
  • No demand for an OTP
  • No pressure to act immediately
  • Uses the official app or website

Quick check: Never share OTPs or passwords, even with someone claiming to be support.
